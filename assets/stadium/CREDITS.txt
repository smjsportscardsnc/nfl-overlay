Night-stadium artwork used in the side panels.

These are illustrations, not photographs. Each one starts from a free Unsplash photo
(Unsplash License, https://unsplash.com/license: free to use and modify, including
commercially, no credit required), is cropped to the panel shape, has team and sponsor
marks taken out, and is then repainted with brush strokes, one shared night palette,
light glow and canvas grain by tools/make_stadium_art.py. 02 is mirrored.
The starting photos are listed here so they can be traced.
