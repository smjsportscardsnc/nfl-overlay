Night-stadium artwork used in the side panels.

These are illustrations in the SMJ brand colours, not photographs. Each one starts from a
free Unsplash photo (Unsplash License, https://unsplash.com/license: free to use and
modify, including commercially, no credit required), is cropped to the panel shape, has
team and sponsor marks taken out, and is then repainted by tools/make_stadium_art.py:
brush strokes, a navy night lit in royal blue with red accent light and silver-white
floodlights (the colours of the logo), light glow and canvas grain. 02 is mirrored.
The starting photos are listed here so they can be traced.
